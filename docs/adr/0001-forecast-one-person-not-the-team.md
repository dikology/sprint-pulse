# Forecast one person, not the team

Sprint Pulse computes Confidence over the Operator's own assigned Issues only. Team Scope is
displayed as an unforecasted total; no confidence is ever attached to it, and there is no toggle
between personal and team modes.

A switchable forecast sounded attractive and was rejected: it doubles every downstream decision —
two velocities, two capacity models, two sets of band thresholds, two mascot states — for a
number the Operator cannot act on alone. "Is the team going to finish?" is a real question, but
it is not one a personal instrument can answer honestly, and answering it badly would undermine
the number that matters.

## Consequences

The aggregation is nonetheless parameterised by *which Issues are mine*, so a team mode remains
buildable later. What is not cheap to reverse is the surrounding model: the Caps, the
Actionable/Waiting split, and the Demonstrated Rate are all defined in terms of one person's
effort, and a team forecast would need its own versions of each.
